## Description
```
Inkwell is a comic editor that runs entirely on your device.

Layout comic pages with professional templates, edit panels, draw, add speech bubbles and text, build characters in Character Studio, and export to PDF — no account required.

Features:
• Comic layout templates (Shonen, Webtoon, grids, and more)
• Panel editor with draw, text, images, and clipart
• Story editor and comic reader
• Local autosave and project backup import/export
• Privacy-first: your comics stay on your device
```
