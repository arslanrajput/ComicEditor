# Privacy Policy — Inkwell

**Last updated:** June 2026

## Introduction

Inkwell ("we," "our," or "the app") is a **comic editor** for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile application.

By using Inkwell, you agree to this policy.

## Summary

- Inkwell works **without a user account**
- Comics, characters, and settings are stored **on your device only**
- We do **not** sell your data or upload your artwork to our servers
- Limited network access may occur for fonts and system share/print features

## Information We Collect

We do **not** collect personal information through Inkwell servers. The app does not require sign-in and does not send your projects to a cloud backend operated by us.

The following may exist **only on your device**:

- Comic project names, layouts, and panel content (drawings, text, speech bubbles)
- Imported photos and images
- Character names, roles, bios, traits, and portraits
- Profile display name, bio, and optional profile photo
- App preferences (page size, grid, export quality, reader mode, story defaults)
- Bookmarked templates and project sort order

## Information We Do Not Collect

- Email addresses or passwords (no account system)
- Precise location
- Contacts, call logs, or SMS
- Advertising identifiers for targeted ads
- Analytics tied to your identity on our servers

## Device Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Camera | Take a photo for a panel or character portrait |
| Photos / Gallery | Import images into panels, projects, or profile |
| Storage | Save projects and export PDF, PNG, or backup files |
| Internet | See Network Use below |

You may deny permissions in device Settings; related features will be unavailable.

## Network Use & Third-Party Services

- **Google Fonts** — font files may be downloaded when you select certain typefaces ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Share & Print** — export uses your device's share/print dialogs; chosen apps may use the network
- **App stores** — updates via Google Play or Apple App Store

We do **not** upload your comic pages or project files to our servers.

## Local Storage & Backups

Projects are saved on-device (Hive). You may export/import JSON backups from Profile. Backups are under your control.

## Data Retention

Data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Children's Privacy

Inkwell is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Parents should supervise minors.

## Security

Data stays on your device. You are responsible for device security and any files you export or share.

## Your Choices

- View, edit, and delete projects and characters in the app
- Export/import backups from Profile
- Revoke permissions in device Settings
- Uninstall to remove all local app data

## International Users

Data is stored locally on your device in the country where you use the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The in-app Privacy Policy screen shows the latest version.
